## Best Practices

### Do:
- ✅ Customize variables for your specific project
- ✅ Iterate with follow-up prompts
- ✅ Validate AI output against real user data
- ✅ Use as starting point, not final output
- ✅ Combine multiple methods for triangulation

### Don't:
- ❌ Use AI output as the "truth" without validation
- ❌ Skip real user interviews entirely
- ❌ Treat personas as real people
- ❌ Present AI-generated insights as direct quotes
- ❌ Forget to cite methods in stakeholder presentations
